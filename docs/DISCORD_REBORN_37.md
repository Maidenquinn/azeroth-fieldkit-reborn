# 🧭 Azeroth FieldKit Reborn — .37
**Clearer previews, better Endless tracking, and support for Reborn 0.6.9.**

### 🖼️ A repaired preview screen
• No more duplicated text or overlapping sample cards.
• Boss health and debuffs stay readable, and controls remain accessible.
• Individual tests open the correct card. **Done** restores your regular HUD.

### ⏱️ Know where you are in Endless
See the game's **next-boss countdown and encounter number** in the HUD, boss list and reminders—with or without a theme.
The timer freezes while paused. Boss names appear when the game reveals them, and charge-based encounters keep their real progress percentage.

### 📊 More useful statistics
• Hover over your stats to see the **current caps provided by the game**.
• Very large Endless damage/healing totals and meter resets are handled correctly, with shorter labels for huge numbers.

### 🌍 Your HUD stays yours
Updated help in all **14 languages**, with your existing profiles and layouts preserved.
