# 🧭 Azeroth FieldKit Reborn — .36 is here!
**A modular HUD, now easier to align — and available in 14 languages.**

### 📐 Arrange your HUD with a live alignment grid
Keep your theme visible while moving your cards, adjust the grid spacing and toggle snapping.
• **Grid on:** snap to grid lines.
• **Grid off:** align card edges and centres with each other and the screen.
• Your overlap settings still apply.

Open **Interface → Layout & arrange → Align HUD**, or use `/fieldkit align`.

### 💬 Move your chat too
Chat automatically unlocks while editing the HUD. Place it where you want, finish editing, and its normal lock returns.

### 🌍 Choose your language
**English · Français · Deutsch · Español · Português (Brasil) · Polski · Čeština · Română · Türkçe · Русский · Українська · 简体中文 · 日本語 · 한국어**

Select a language in **Preferences**, or choose **Automatic** to follow the game. The interface updates immediately, with or without a theme, using fonts that support each alphabet.
*Names provided by the game stay in the game's language. Translation wording feedback is welcome!*

### 🛠️ Fixes & polish
• Fixed minimap scaling inside themed frames, with better refresh after moving, resizing or changing screen size.
• Improved compatibility with older HUD profiles.
• Refined translations and tooltip font handling.

**Your existing settings and layouts are kept.**
Open `/fieldkit` to customize your HUD, and share your screenshots or feedback!
